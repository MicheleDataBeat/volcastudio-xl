      Guide · the short version
      
    
      Choose your samples → prepare the kit → transfer. Everything else is optional.
      
  
      
  
      
    
      
      
        
        
      1. Gathering: use “Add samples…” or “Add a folder…” to import copies, or “Manage libraries…” to register a folder in place. Choose “All samples” to search every library.
        2. 
        
      2. Select samples (Shift/Ctrl/⌘-click), press Enter to add them to the workbench. Reorder with ↑ ↓ or drag. Listen with Space.
        3. 
        
      3. Optional: J/K lower or raise by 0–99 semitones (pitch and duration together). “Make ×2 variant” adds a version one octave higher and half as long, keeping existing kit entries. “Align keys…” proposes shifts to match a reference sample’s pitch class.
        4. 
        
      4. Name the kit and “Save as new kit”. A kit holds up to 100 samples and about 65 seconds, measured on the final audio.
        5. 
        
      5. Connections: choose the output wired to SYNC IN and disconnect speakers and headphones from it. Sending a kit clears all 100 sample slots. Choose “Send saved kit to Volca…”, review the summary and confirm when ready.
        6. 
      
      
      
      For listening, choose Source audio or Volca preview. Both use Chrome’s sound output. Autoplay selection listens as you browse; turn it off before a transfer.
      
      
      Download SYRO files… keeps the prepared files as a ZIP without sending. These are data-audio signals; do not audition them. Memory also exports signals for selected pattern memories or a snapshot with an explicit destination.
      
    
      
    
      
      
      What the messages mean
      
      
      
        
      Saved
      Written on this computer only.
      
        
      Checked offline
      Signals were encoded and verified, then discarded. Nothing was played.
      
        
      Signals sent
      The Browser App finished playing the signals. The SYNC IN connection carries no acknowledgement; check the result on the instrument.
      
        
      Stopped while sending
      Part of the data may have arrived. Check affected samples or pattern memories before resending. For interrupted firmware updates, consult the DataBeat OS manual and the firmware guide.
